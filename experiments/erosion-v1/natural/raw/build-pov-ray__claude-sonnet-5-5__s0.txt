**Change:** Raise the per-turn command batch limit from 4 to 8, and tell the executor to pack related steps (write, build, test) into one turn.

**Rationale:** In the failed run, turns were the binding constraint. `build-pov-ray` used all 40 turns but only 45 of the 80 environment calls, and 19 tasks ended in `budget_exhausted`, 9 of them with reward=1 before they could be verified. Larger batches convert the unused environment-call budget into more progress per turn, and the harness still stops a batch at the first failure, so the risk is limited.

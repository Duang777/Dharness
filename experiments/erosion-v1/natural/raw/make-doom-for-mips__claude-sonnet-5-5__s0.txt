This is untested. I chose it because turns, not environment calls, look like the binding budget: the failed run used 33 turns but only 30 environment calls, against caps of 40 and 80. Raising the batch limit from 4 to 8 commands per execute turn, and telling the model to combine edit, build and test steps into one batch, should let it fit more work into the same 40 turns. That could convert some of the 19 budget-exhausted runs, since 9 of them already reached reward 1 without finishing verification.

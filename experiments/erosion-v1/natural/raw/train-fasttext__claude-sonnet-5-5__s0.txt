Rationale: the failed `train-fasttext` run, and likely other `budget_exhausted` failures, hit the wall clock or call limits while long jobs such as training and builds ran in the foreground under the 300s command cap, leaving no valid artifact when the budget ran out. I changed the executor rules to tell the agent to run long jobs in the background and poll them with short sleeps, and to produce a minimal valid deliverable early, so the final state is usable whenever the run ends. The diff below is untested.

Rationale: explicit `repeat_reason` polls are currently allowed by the immediate repeat check but still trip cycle/stagnation recovery, wasting turns and forcing replans while agents wait for services or setup. This persists `repeat_reason` on receipts and exempts deliberate read-only polls from recovery, preventing false replans and likely converting budget-exhausted tasks into successes.

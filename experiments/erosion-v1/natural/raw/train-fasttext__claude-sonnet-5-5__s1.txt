**Rationale:** Many failures end in `budget_exhausted` well before the 40-turn limit (train-fasttext used only 20 turns and 24 environment calls). That points to wall-clock time lost to long foreground commands, such as training runs that hit the 300s command timeout, being retried, or being followed by slow polling. The change adds executor guidance to run long jobs in the background with a log file, poll briefly, size workloads to the remaining wall time, and leave a working artifact in place early, so the final state is usable even if the budget runs out.

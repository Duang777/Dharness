Currently a completion rejection can grant another repair while `finalization_repair_used` remains true from a previous finalization repair, so `_allowed_actions` prevents the model from actually executing the newly granted repair. Resetting that flag when a repair is granted aligns the action policy with the repair budget and lets the model use all permitted repair attempts instead of being forced toward budget exhaustion.

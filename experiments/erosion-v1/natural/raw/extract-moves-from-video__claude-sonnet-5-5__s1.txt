Rationale: Many failures are `budget_exhausted` runs that never reached a finish. Nine of those still scored reward=1, which suggests the work was often done but the run ran out of time before it was wrapped up. Asking for an early complete deliverable, fast sampled commands and larger batches should convert more of those near-misses into passes without touching the completion-control logic.

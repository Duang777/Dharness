**Rationale:** With only 3 reserved turns, a finish that fails mechanical or semantic review can't be repaired and re-verified before the turn or wall-clock limit. I suspect this is why runs that already solved the task still ended as `budget_exhausted`. I haven't confirmed that against the run journals. Reserving 6 turns and 15% of wall time moves the agent into finalization earlier, which leaves room for finish, a repair batch, and a second finish with review. The cost is that tasks still unsolved at that point lose some working turns.

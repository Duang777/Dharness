**Rationale:** Many runs, including build-pov-ray, spend all 40 turns on work and never reach a finish/verify cycle. A 3-turn reserve is too small for a finish, a rejection, a repair batch and a second finish, so even runs that had already solved the task ran out of turns. Starting finalization at 6 turns remaining forces a finish attempt earlier, and allowing one last execute batch during finalization (even with no prior findings) keeps unfinished tasks from losing their final work step.

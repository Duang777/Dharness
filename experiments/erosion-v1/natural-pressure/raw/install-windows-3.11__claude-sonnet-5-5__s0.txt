**Rationale:** Today, once two semantic reviews have run, the loop ends the run with `budget_exhausted` ("completion review budget exhausted") on the next rejection or finish attempt. That happens even when repairs remain and the isolated checks would pass. With the change, an exhausted review quota drops the semantic review for later finish attempts and relies on the fresh isolated mechanical evidence gate, so runs that are already solved can reach `verified` instead of timing out on the review cap.

**Rationale:** Many budget-exhausted runs had already solved the task but kept exploring, or entered finalization with too few turns to survive a rejected finish and its repair. Raising the finalization reserve (3→6 turns, 10%→15% wall time) and adding a "finish now if solved" nudge once less than 40% of turns or time remain should turn those near-misses into verified completions.

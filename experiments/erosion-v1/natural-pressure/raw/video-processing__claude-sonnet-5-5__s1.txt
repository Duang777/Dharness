Rationale: Runs were ending as `budget_exhausted` as soon as the second semantic review rejected, or whenever the review quota was spent. That happened even when the task was already solved and the isolated mechanical checks could still pass. With this change, once the review quota is used up, the model gets repair turns and a later `finish` is accepted on the isolated, fresh, mechanically checked evidence alone, with the downgrade recorded in the journal. This should convert some of the 9 solved-but-exhausted runs into verified completions.

Rationale:
Rejected completion attempts currently consume the environment-call reserve, so the loop often starts the next iteration already over budget and reports `budget_exhausted` before a repair can be attempted. Refunding only the failed verification calls when a repair is granted, and hiding repair execution when no work capacity remains, lets already-solved tasks submit a fresh completion check set instead of being cut off.
